The BCP bearings (BCPxxEX) are proprietary plummer block assemblies used throughout Silo Cluster 3 and the Mill area. They are not standard SKF bearings and are not in the All Parts catalogue — they need to be sourced directly from BCP.

## 3. Recommended Stocking List — "4 of Each"

Bearings that appear on 2 or more conveyors, stocked by All Parts. Ordered by priority.

| Priority | Designation | Type | Bore×OD×W | Locations | Conveyors | Notes | Qty |
|----------|-------------|------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------|-----|
| ⭐⭐⭐ | **22215 E** | Spherical roller | 75×130×31mm | 26 | 8 | Most common on site (BB4–9, RB8–9, MC2) | **4** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ | **22220 E** | Spherical roller | 100×180×46mm | 4 | 4 | Boot pulleys on ELV1–4 — **main elevators are critical plant** | **4+** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ | **22212 E** | Spherical roller | 60×110×28mm | 9 | 6 | All RB5–7 bend/snub/GTU pulleys and Silo Cluster 1 (BB1–3 GTU) | **4** |
| ⭐⭐ | **22215 EK** | Spherical roller | 75×130×31mm | 2 | 2 | Same bore as 22215 E, tapered bore version — interchangeable with sleeve | **4** |
| ⭐⭐ | **22216 EK** | Spherical roller | 80×140×33mm | 3 | 3 | BB1–3 snub pulleys (Silo Cluster 1) | **4** |
| ⭐⭐ | **22217 E** | Spherical roller | 85×150×36mm | 2 | 2 | RB8 and RB9 GTU pulleys | **4** |

**On the 22220 E:** The boot pulleys on ELV1–4 are the most critical point on site. A failed boot pulley bearing stops the elevator and could halt the entire terminal. Recommend holding at least 4 on the shelf and reviewing whether 6 is more appropriate.

**On 22215 E / 22215 EK:** These are effectively the same bearing in different bore configurations. You could argue for 8 total rather than 4+4, split across the two variants.

## 4. Gaps — Bearings on Site but Not in All Parts Catalogue

These bearings are actively in use on site but not currently available through All Parts. This is a potential expansion opportunity.

| Designation | Instances | Conveyors | Where used |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **22513 E** | 13 | 7 | RB1–4 (Receiving) and RB5–7 (Silo 1) — snub, tail, bend, tension pulleys |
| **22520 E** | 9 | 6 | RB5–7 drive pulleys; TR5, TR6, TR7 (trippers) head and bend pulleys |
| **22516 E** | 6 | 3 | BB1–3 bend pulleys (Silo Cluster 1) |
| **22518 E** | 4 | 4 | RB1–4 drive pulleys (Receiving) |
| **22522 E** | 3 | 3 | BB1–3 drive pulleys (Silo Cluster 1) |
| **22515 E** | 3 | 3 | BB1–3 tail pulleys (Silo Cluster 1) |
| **22511 E** | 2 | 2 | RB3 and RB4 snub pulleys (Receiving) |
| **1220 K** | 1 | 1 | MC2 drive pulley (mill conveyor) |

**The 22513 E in particular is worth adding to the catalogue.** It's on 7 conveyors including the main receiving belts (RB1–4) which take grain directly from the ship unloader. If one goes down, it's a significant disruption.

The 225xx series as a whole (22511–22522) covers a large chunk of the site's conveyor pulleys. All Parts currently stocks the 222xx series — extending into 225xx would significantly improve coverage for this site.
