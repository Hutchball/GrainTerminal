## Summary
MCC13 primarily controls the tripper systems (Trippers 8 & 9) and receiving chain conveyors (RCC3 and RCC4) for Silo 3 operations at the Seaforth Grain Terminal. The system includes comprehensive valve control for the receiving conveyors and coordinated tripper control with anti-coincidence interlocking.
