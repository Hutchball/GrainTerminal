Health & Safety

⚠
Grain terminals are high-risk environments.

Dust explosion, engulfment, confined spaces, and machinery hazards require specific controls and competent personnel.

Primary Hazards in Grain Terminals

HIGH RISK

Dust Explosion

Grain dust is explosive. Concentrations between LEL and UEL with ignition source can cause catastrophic explosion.

Controls: ATEX zoning, explosion relief, dust extraction, earthing, hot work permits
HIGH RISK

Engulfment/Entrapment

Personnel can be engulfed in flowing grain within seconds. Rescue is extremely difficult.

Controls: Lockout/tagout, entry permits, rescue equipment, never walk on grain surface
MEDIUM RISK

Confined Spaces

Silos, bins, and enclosed conveyors are confined spaces with atmosphere and access hazards.

Controls: Permit to work, atmosphere testing, rescue plan, trained personnel
MEDIUM RISK

Machinery

Conveyors, elevators, and rotating equipment present entanglement and crushing hazards.

Controls: Guarding, interlocks, lockout/tagout, maintenance procedures
MEDIUM RISK

Working at Height

Silo tops, conveyor gantries, and elevated platforms.

Controls: Edge protection, harnesses, rescue plan, competent persons
MEDIUM RISK

Fumigants

Phosphine and other fumigants used for pest control are highly toxic.

Controls: Specialist contractors, exclusion zones, gas monitoring, emergency procedures

Key HSE Requirements for Grain Handling

From HSG103 and HSE Food Industry guidance. Source: HSE

Requirement Detail
Building Design Locate in open air or strong steel-framed building with lightweight panel walls for explosion relief
Silos/Bins Must be fitted with explosion relief venting to unoccupied place of safety (preferably outside)
Bucket Elevators Must be equipped with explosion relief at head and as close as practicable to boot
Dust Collectors Open bag filter units must be totally enclosed and fitted with explosion relief
Rotary Valves Only type-tested, ATEX-certified rotary valves as explosion chokes
Assessment Review Every 3-5 years minimum, immediate reassessment after any process change

Statutory Training Requirements

Personnel working in grain terminals typically require:

  • ATEX/DSEAR awareness for all personnel
  • Confined space entry and rescue (where applicable)
  • Working at height
  • Lockout/tagout procedures
  • Manual handling
  • First aid (appropriate level)
  • Fire safety and emergency procedures
  • Site-specific induction

About this System

This knowledge base is built for engineers and managers working at Seaforth Grain Terminal. It provides a single searchable reference for mechanical and electrical equipment, conveyor roller codes, dust control systems, switchroom drawings, and site safety information.

All data is sourced from verified site documents, drawings, and manufacturer records. Every entry carries a confidence level — VERIFIED, LIKELY, UNCERTAIN, or UNKNOWN — so users know exactly how much to trust each piece of information.
